# Kupal VPN Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 26, 2026**

Kupal VPN is an Android VPN client maintained by the app publisher.

## Information handled by the app

- **VPN configuration:** Server addresses, protocol settings, payload templates, and credentials that you enter are stored locally on your device so the app can establish the connection you configure.
- **VPN connection data:** The app uses Android's VPN service to route network traffic through the server you select. The operator of that server may be able to observe connection metadata and traffic that is not protected by end-to-end encryption. Use only servers you trust.
- **Advertising and consent:** Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interaction data, and consent choices to serve and measure ads and comply with privacy requirements. Rewarded ads are optional. VPN time is credited only after Google reports that the reward was earned.
- **Diagnostics and analytics:** Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect app usage events, device/app information, and crash diagnostics to help understand app performance and fix errors.

## Sharing

The app does not sell the VPN credentials you enter. Google advertising, consent, analytics, and crash-reporting services process data under their own terms and privacy policies. A VPN server you choose is operated by its own provider and has its own privacy practices.

## Your choices

You can manage advertising consent through the in-app **Settings → Privacy options** entry point when Google UMP indicates that it is required. You can disable optional rewarded-ad access in the app. You can also stop using the VPN and remove locally stored profiles by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

## Security

Keep your VPN passwords, private keys, UUIDs, and subscription links private. Do not place personal credentials in public server or configuration repositories.

## Children's privacy

Kupal VPN is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as app features and service providers change. For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the support channel listed on the app's distribution page.
